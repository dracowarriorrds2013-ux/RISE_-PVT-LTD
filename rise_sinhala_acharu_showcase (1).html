Authentic Preparation

Crafted with Heritage Spices

Unlike oil-heavy pickles, **Rise Sinhala Acharu** is rooted in authentic Sri Lankan culinary tradition—using coconut vinegar, stone-ground yellow mustard, and sun-soaked spices to deliver an irresistible crunch.

🧅

Red Shallots (රතු ළූණු)

Whole peeled red shallots preserved intact to maintain their natural crunch, absorbing mustard vinegar deep into every layer.

🌶️

Green Chilies (අමු මිරිස්)

Hand-picked local green chilies slit down the middle to release a sharp, fiery warmth into the pickle broth.

🥕

Crisp Carrots & Papaya

Julienned carrots and raw green papaya add vibrant color and an ultra-satisfying crispy bite to every spoonful.

🌾

Ground Yellow Mustard

Stone-ground yellow mustard seed paste provides the signature pungent kick and rich golden coating.

🥥

Pure Coconut Vinegar

Naturally fermented coconut vinegar provides crisp acidity and acts as a natural preservative with zero added oils.

🧂

Black Pepper & Salt

Whole cracked black peppercorns and sea salt balance the sweetness of red shallots and the sharp vinegar tang.
